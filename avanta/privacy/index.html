Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026
Last updated: November 10, 2026

Who we are

Avanta is a product of Diya Technologies, Inc. ("Diya Technologies," "we," "us"). Avanta is a clinician-directed clinical documentation assistant: a clinician captures a visit, reviews and confirms the clinical content, and receives an unsigned draft note to review, edit, and sign in their own EHR.

What this policy covers

This policy covers website and account data: information about people who visit this website, contact us, or hold an Avanta account.

Patient data is not covered by this policy. Patient information that a clinician or practice uses with Avanta is governed by that practice's agreements with us, including a business associate agreement where required, and by the practice's own Notice of Privacy Practices.

Current status

Avanta is currently in development and is not processing real patient data yet.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you send us, such as your name, email address, practice name, and the content of your message, for example when you ask about early access.
  • Account information, such as your name, email address, practice name, and role, if you are given access to Avanta.
  • Basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, which our hosting and service providers receive when you visit the website or use the service, and which we use to operate and secure it.

How we use information

We use website and account information to operate, secure, and support the website and Avanta, to respond to you, and to manage early access. We do not sell it.

Who we share information with

  • Service providers under contract that help us operate the website and the service, such as hosting and email providers.
  • Others when required by law or to protect our rights, and a successor in a merger, acquisition, or sale of the business.

We do not sell personal information.

Patient data and EHR data

When Avanta begins processing patient data, we will use and disclose it only as permitted by our agreement with the practice, including a business associate agreement where required, and applicable law. That includes sharing it with service providers under contract that help us operate the service. It will never be sold.

When a clinician connects Avanta to an EHR, EHR data is accessed read-only, used only to draft documentation for the requesting clinician, and handled in line with the EHR or API provider's developer terms, such as Office Ally's developer terms when the connection runs through Office Ally. Naming a provider here does not indicate a partnership or endorsement.

Do Not Track

This website does not track visitors over time or across third-party websites, and it does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Because there is no tracking to turn off, the website does not respond differently to browser Do Not Track signals. We do not knowingly allow third parties to collect information about your online activity across other websites through this website.

Children

Avanta and this website are intended for healthcare professionals and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Reviewing or correcting your information

You can ask us to review or correct the contact or account information you have given us by emailing the address below.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date at the top. If a change is material, we will also notify account holders by email.
