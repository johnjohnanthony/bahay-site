Your judgment

The clinician stays in control

  • Avanta never signs notes.
  • Avanta does not bill, code, order, or prescribe.
  • Clinical judgment and the final note stay with you.

Data handling

Privacy and security

Avanta is being built so that clinicians control what goes into a note. It is currently in development and is not processing real patient data yet.

When Avanta begins processing patient data, we will use and disclose it only as permitted by our agreement with the practice, including a business associate agreement where required, and applicable law. That includes sharing it with service providers under contract that help us operate the service. It will never be sold. EHR data will be accessed read-only and used only to draft documentation for the requesting clinician.

Questions about privacy or security? Email privacy@diyatechai.com. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms.
