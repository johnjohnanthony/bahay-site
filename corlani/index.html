Data handling

Privacy and security

Corlani is being built so that clinicians control what goes into a note. It is currently in development and is not processing real patient data yet.

When Corlani begins processing patient data, we will use and disclose it only as permitted by our agreement with the practice, including a business associate agreement where required, and applicable law. That includes sharing it with service providers under contract that help us operate the service. It will never be sold. EHR data will be accessed read-only and used only to draft documentation for the requesting clinician.

Questions about privacy or security? Email corlani@diyatechai.com. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms.
